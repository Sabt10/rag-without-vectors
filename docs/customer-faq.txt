Customer FAQ

Q: When will the new dashboard be available?
A: We are rolling it out in phases, starting with internal teams and then customer accounts that use the standard deployment path.

Q: Will the update change permissions?
A: Permissions remain compatible, but new access roles are visible in the admin panel to support the refreshed release workflow.

Q: Do I need to do anything before the release?
A: No action is required for most customers. If you use custom automation, review your schedule before the maintenance window.
